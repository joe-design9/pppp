On PC & iPhone

Not announced  Rockstar has not announced a PC version yet. GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 both reached PC after their console launches, so a later port is likely but unconfirmed.

  1. Use this hub in any desktop browser. The countdown and checklist work in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.
  2. Install it as an app. In Chrome or Edge, use the install icon in the address bar to pin Leonida Hub to your taskbar.
  3. Watch for the PC port. Follow Rockstar Newswire for a Rockstar Games Launcher or Steam listing.

GTA VI itself is not coming to iPhone. Leonida Hub is. Pick an install method below.

Get the app

Easiest · 20 seconds

Home Screen app

Works on any iPhone and on PC. No Mac, no account.

From a Windows PC · 10 min

Native app via Sideloadly

Download LeonidaHub.ipa from the project's GitHub build, open it in Sideloadly, plug in your iPhone and sign in with your Apple ID.

From a Mac · 5 min

Native app via Xcode

Double-click LeonidaHub.xcodeproj, choose your Apple ID under Signing, pick your iPhone and press Run.
